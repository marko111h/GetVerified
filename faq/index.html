Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what clients ask us most before integrating.

What messaging channels does Get Verified support?

We support SMS (A2P), WhatsApp Business, Viber, and RCS messaging, along with HLR lookup and number verification services.

Which countries do you cover?

We offer global coverage, reaching virtually every country worldwide through our carrier network.

How do I get API access?

Reach out via our Contact page or email sales@get-verified.net with your expected use case and volume. We'll provide your API credentials and onboarding host after a short setup call.

Is there a free trial or test credits?

Yes, we offer a free trial. Contact our sales team to get started with test credits.

GET vs POST — which should I use for the API?

Both work identically for sending messages. We recommend POST with HTTPS and an Authorization header for production use, since GET requests can expose credentials in server logs. See our API documentation for details.

How fast is message delivery?

Delivery time depends on the destination network, but our intelligent routing typically delivers messages within seconds under normal conditions.

Do you support Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) / OTP use cases?

Yes — our Verifications service is built specifically for one-time password delivery and identity verification via SMS.

How is billing calculated?

Billing is flexible — we support both postpaid and prepaid arrangements, depending on what works best for your business.
